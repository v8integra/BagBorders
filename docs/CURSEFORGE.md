## BagBorders

**All your bags in one window, and every bag in its own color.**

BagBorders replaces Blizzard's bag and bank windows with one clean window each.
Every bag gets its own border color and a free-slot count, so you can tell at a
glance where things are and which bag is full.

### Bags
- Backpack, bags, reagent bag and keyring in one window
- A color and a free-slot count for each bag
- Search that dims everything that doesn't match
- One-click Sort
- A bag strip: hover a bag to light up its slots, click to hide or show them, drag
  to swap bags
- Item level on gear, colored by quality

### Bank
- Every bank tab as its own colored section, with free counts
- Bank-bag slots, Clean Up Bank and Deposit all
- An account bank view
- A "Blizzard bank" button for buying slots and tabs

### Alts
- Remembers every character's bags, bank and gold
- View any character's bags or bank, or the account bank, from anywhere (read only)
- Item tooltips show how many each character has

### Options
`/bagborders`, or Options > AddOns > BagBorders: columns, scale, opacity, lock, and
every feature on/off.

### Safe in combat
BagBorders lets Blizzard decide when bags open and close, and never changes
Blizzard's own code or values. You can use items from the window in combat, and
the bank keeps working.

### Good to know
- Each character appears in the alts viewer after you log in on it once, and its
  bank after you open the bank there once.
- To put things in the account bank, drag them onto an account slot. Right-click
  deposits go to the character bank.
- Prefer Blizzard's bags? Turn the windows off on the options page. With Blizzard's
  combined bags you still get the colored borders and free counts.
