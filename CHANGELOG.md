# Changelog

## 0.9.0 (2026-11-05)

First release for the WoW: Forever launch (UI build 1.60.1 70790).

- New all-in-one bag window (combined and individual bag modes), with per-bag
  colored borders and free-slot counts.
- New bank window: tabs as sections, bank-bag slots, Clean Up Bank, Deposit all,
  an account bank view, and a button to switch to Blizzard's bank panel.
- Search, Sort, a bag strip (hover, hide or swap bags), and item level on gear.
- Alts: saved bags, bank and gold for every character, a read-only viewer, and
  per-character item counts in tooltips.
- An options page under Options > AddOns, opened with `/bagborders`.
- Fixed: the bank window went blank with BagBorders enabled on the launch build.
- With Blizzard's bags: the reagent bag docks next to the combined window and opens
  with Toggle Backpack.
- All on-screen text can be translated (`Locales/`).

Known: the alts features are mock-tested but not yet checked in game with more
than one character.
